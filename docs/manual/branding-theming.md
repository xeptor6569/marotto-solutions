# Branding, theming & public site

## Business profile

**Settings → Business** is the single source of your identity — name, legal name, tagline, phone, email, address, service area, and timezone. Nothing brand-specific is hardcoded: change it here and the whole app follows, including printed documents and outgoing email.

## Appearance

**Settings → Appearance** controls the look. A live preview on the right (above the settings on a phone) shows your choices, including the document letterhead, before you save; flip it between light and dark to check both.

- **Look** sets typefaces, corner shape, spacing, and surfaces across the admin, setup, sign-in, and client pages:
  - **Studio** — clean sans-serif with soft shadows (the default).
  - **Ledger** — serif headings, hairline rules, and small-caps labels, like good paperwork.
  - **Instrument** — dense and precise, with monospaced figures and a graph-paper background.
  - **Workshop** — bold, chunky headings, roomier spacing, and a warm background.
  - **Soft** — frosted panels, rounded controls, and a gentle tinted glow.
- **Color** picks the accent and gray palette. Choosing a Look suggests a matching color, which you can change. **Custom** lets you pick your own accent, gray, and corner radius.
- **Density** makes the whole interface a notch more compact or roomier than the Look's default.
- **Default appearance** sets light/dark/system for new visitors; everyone can still toggle for themselves.
- **Logo** uploads appear in the admin sidebar, sign-in page, client share pages, public site header — and optionally on printed documents instead of the text letterhead.
- **Letterhead** lines and the **document accent color** shape printed invoices, estimates, quotes, receipts, and contracts. The letterhead typeface follows the Look. Documents always print light-on-white regardless of screen theme.

## Public site

**Settings → Public Site** manages the marketing homepage: hero text, search-engine metadata, "why choose us" points, your service catalog (each service gets a homepage card, a detail page, and a quote-form option), and testimonials. Sections hide automatically when empty.

Prefer no public site? Turn it off and your homepage becomes a simple branded card with a sign-in link.
