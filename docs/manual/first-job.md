# Your first job, start to finish

This walkthrough follows one piece of work through the app. Skip any step you don't need — every document can also be created on its own.

## 1. Add the client

**Create → Client**, then enter a name plus the email and phone you'll send documents to. If the request came in through your website's quote form, the client already exists as a **prospect** — open it from **Clients** and use **Mark as client** once they're a real customer.

## 2. Create a job

**Create → Job** and pick the client. The job page becomes the home for everything about this work: its documents, photos and PDFs, time tracking, and notes.

Create documents *from the job page* (its **Create** button) so they're linked to the job and pre-filled with the client's details.

## 3. Send an estimate or quote

From the job, **Create → Estimate** (or **Quote**). Add line items — or pick a **preset** to fill them in — and save. [Estimates, quotes, invoices & receipts](documents.md) covers the editor in detail.

- Offering choices? Add **packages** (e.g. Basic vs Premium) or **material choices** (e.g. Hardwood vs Laminate). Each one is a row you can open, reorder, or duplicate. Mark the package and the choice you suggest as **Recommended**. The client sees every option with its price, and the total includes those recommended picks until you record their decision.
- Proposing extras the client hasn't agreed to? Tick **Needs client approval** on those lines; they're shown separately as "pending your approval".
- Add **estimated hours** to compare against the time you actually log on the job.

On the saved document, use **Share** to copy the client link or **Email** to send it. The client can view and print it without an account.

## 4. Record the client's decision

When the client says yes, open the estimate and, if it has packages or choices, mark what they picked under **Select options** and **Save selection**. Change the **Status** to reflect where it stands, and drag it along the **Board view** (Backlog → To Do → In Progress → Done) as work progresses.

## 5. Take a deposit (optional)

Two ways, pick whichever suits you:

- **One invoice, deposit recorded against it (simplest):** convert to an invoice now (next step), send it, and when the deposit arrives use **Record payment** with type **Down payment**. The invoice shows what's been paid and what's left.
- **Separate deposit invoice:** on the estimate or quote, open the actions menu and choose **Deposit invoice** to bill a percentage or fixed amount on its own. The final invoice you convert later bills the full amount, so add a line with a negative price for the deposit already received.

## 6. Invoice the work

On the estimate or quote, open the actions menu (**⋯**) and choose **Convert → To invoice**. Line items and the selected options carry over (if lines are still pending approval, you'll be asked whether to include them). Set a **due date**, review the payment methods shown on the invoice, and save. Then **Share** or **Email** it.

## 7. Get paid

When money arrives, open the invoice and use **Record payment** in the Payments panel ([more on payments](payments.md)). A **receipt** is created automatically and the balance updates; at zero the invoice is marked **paid**. If you've set up Stripe, clients can pay by card from the shared link and it's recorded for you.

Keep an eye on the dashboard's **Outstanding** and **Overdue** figures for anything still unpaid.

## 8. Wrap up

Log your hours in the job's **Time** panel, attach before/after photos under **Attachments**, and if helpers worked the job, record their payouts. Everything stays together on the job page for next time.
